01 / The methods

Six lenses on rock quality

Each system uses different evidence and serves a different design decision. Record the source and version of every rating in a real investigation.

Core logging

RQD

Rock Quality Designation is the percentage of a core run made up of sound core pieces at least 100 mm long. It describes core fragmentation and is an input to several other systems.

RQD = 100 × Σ(length of qualifying pieces) / run length
General / tunnels

RMR

Bieniawski's 1989 Rock Mass Rating adds ratings for intact strength, RQD, discontinuity spacing and condition, groundwater, then adjusts for discontinuity orientation.

RMR = UCS + RQD + spacing + condition + water + orientation
Underground support

Q-system

NGI's Q-system combines block size, joint shear resistance, and water/stress effects. Its logarithmic quality index is used with opening size and ESR to assess tunnel support.

Q = (RQD/Jn) × (Jr/Ja) × (Jw/SRF)
Strength estimation

GSI

The Geological Strength Index is assessed from rock mass structure and discontinuity surface condition. Its chart-based range helps estimate Hoek–Brown rock mass properties.

GSI ≈ chart(structure, surface condition)
Rock slopes

Q-slope

Q-slope adapts Q parameters to slope orientation and environmental effects. An empirical relation estimates an unsupported slope angle within its calibration domain.

Q-slope = (RQD/Jn) × (Jr/Ja)ₒ × (Jwice/SRFslope)
Mine design

MRMR

Laubscher's Mining Rock Mass Rating starts with his in situ rating, then applies mining adjustments for weathering, joint orientation, blasting and induced stress.

MRMR = IRMR × Aweather × Aorientation × Ablast × Astress
Important: RMR (Bieniawski) and IRMR (Laubscher) use different rating tables. Do not feed an RMR score directly into the MRMR adjustment. GSI and Q-slope results here are educational estimates, not a substitute for field mapping or design checks.

NGI Q-system

Enter ratings from the NGI Q-system parameter tables. Joint roughness and alteration should describe the critical joint set. If measured RQD is below 10, Q uses a nominal RQD of 10.

For tunnel support, Q must be used with span/height, Excavation Support Ratio (ESR), and the applicable NGI support chart.

GSI visual estimate

Select the observed structure and joint surface quality. This simplified matrix gives an indicative range; map the actual rock mass on the published GSI chart for engineering use.

GSI is unsuitable as a standalone check for structurally controlled planar or wedge failure.

Q-slope screening

Use slope-specific orientation, environmental and stress factors. The output angle is an empirical screening estimate for unsupported slopes, not a factor of safety.

Use the Q-slope orientation table for the relevant failure mode.

For wedges, the published method can use both critical joint sets. This simplified calculator uses one representative Jr/Ja and O factor. Verify kinematics, slope height, water and site performance separately.

Laubscher MRMR

Enter an IRMR calculated using Laubscher's tables, then apply mining adjustment factors as fractions (1 = no reduction). Do not enter Bieniawski RMR here.

Laubscher's published mining design charts and the applicable MRMR version are required to interpret the adjusted rating.
